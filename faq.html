FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about MenoBrief.

Is MenoBrief free?

Yes. MenoBrief is free to use. No account or payment is required.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use the application without registration.

Is my health information uploaded?

No. The application processes the information locally in your browser; it does not send patient information to a MenoBrief backend, server, AI service or third party.

What happens when I refresh or close the page?

Your temporary working information is cleared. You will need to enter it again if you want to create another briefing.

How do I get my briefing?

Complete the four steps, review your information, and download the generated PDF. The PDF is created locally on your device.

Can I use MenoBrief on a mobile phone?

Yes. MenoBrief works in a mobile browser and the layout adjusts to smaller screens. You can also install it as a progressive web app (PWA) on supported devices.

Can I review my PDF before downloading?

Yes. Step 4 shows your structured clinical briefing on screen before you generate and download the PDF.

Does MenoBrief diagnose menopause?

No. MenoBrief is an appointment preparation and communication tool, not a diagnostic or treatment tool. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

Does MenoBrief use AI?

No. There is no active AI integration. Information is organised locally using the application's existing logic.

Where is my downloaded PDF stored?

It is saved through your browser's normal download process to your device. The exact location depends on your browser's download settings.

Is perimenopause the same as menopause?

No. Perimenopause is the time leading up to menopause, when periods and hormone levels begin to change. Menopause is reached after 12 consecutive months without a period (not using hormonal contraception). Both are natural stages of reproductive ageing.

How can I prepare for my medical appointment?

Track your symptoms, menstrual history and medications, and note questions to discuss. MenoBrief organises this into a doctor-friendly PDF briefing you can take to your appointment.

Does MenoBrief determine my menopause stage?

No. MenoBrief does not calculate or assign a menopause or STRAW+10 stage. It is an appointment preparation tool only; any staging or diagnosis should be made by a qualified healthcare professional.

Does MenoBrief use the STRAW+10 framework?

MenoBrief references STRAW+10 for educational purposes only. It does not calculate, determine or assign a STRAW+10 stage.

Can I track my symptoms with MenoBrief?

Yes. Step 1 lets you select symptoms and describe your experience in your own words. For ongoing tracking, keep a symptom diary and bring it to your appointment.
