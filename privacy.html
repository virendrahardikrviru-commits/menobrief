PRIVACY & SAFETY

Privacy & Safety

MenoBrief is designed to help people prepare for healthcare appointments while keeping their briefing information in the browser during use.

1. Local Processing

Information entered into MenoBrief is processed locally in your browser. The application does not send patient information to a MenoBrief backend or database.

2. Temporary Browser Memory

Your working information is held temporarily while you use the application. Refreshing the page or closing it clears the active working session. MenoBrief does not provide automatic long-term storage of your entries.

3. No Account Required

You can use MenoBrief without creating an account or registering.

4. PDF Generation and Downloads

Your briefing PDF is generated locally in your browser. When you download it, the file is saved using your browser's normal download process. The downloaded PDF remains on your device unless you choose to share or move it.

5. Patient Inputs Processed Locally

The information you enter into MenoBrief — your symptoms, menstrual history, medication details and free-text narrative — is processed locally in your browser. It is held in browser memory only while you use the page and is not sent to a MenoBrief backend, database, AI service or third party. Refreshing or closing the page clears your entries.

6. Local PDF Generation

Your clinical briefing PDF is generated locally on your device using a locally hosted copy of the jsPDF library. The PDF is created in your browser and downloaded through your browser's normal download process. The downloaded PDF remains on your device unless you choose to share or move it. MenoBrief does not retain a copy.

7. Public Website Sharing

The "Share MenoBrief" buttons promote the public MenoBrief website only. They share the public page link and a short description of the tool. They never access your patient inputs, questionnaire responses or generated PDF, and they never send patient information anywhere. Sharing opens in your chosen app (for example, email or a messaging app) subject to that app's own terms.

8. Ordinary Technical Information

Like most websites, the MenoBrief site is served by website hosting infrastructure that processes ordinary technical information such as your IP address, browser type and page requests as part of delivering the page. MenoBrief does not control this hosting infrastructure and does not specify or guarantee any particular log-retention period. This technical information is not linked to your patient inputs, which are never transmitted to MenoBrief.

9. AI and Analytics

MenoBrief has no active AI integration and no analytics or tracking. Your briefing information is organised using the application's own local logic. No patient data is sent to any AI service.

10. Important Privacy Limitations

Local browser processing does not guarantee complete security or anonymity. Information held in browser memory can be inspected through browser developer tools, and any script running on the page can access in-memory state. Protect your device and any downloaded PDFs, particularly when they contain sensitive health information.

11. Your Responsibility

Enter only information you are comfortable processing on your device. Keep your browser and device secure, refresh or close the page when finished, and store downloaded PDFs carefully.
